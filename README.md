# Orphonos

Orphonos is a mobile application designed to empower individuals who have lost their voice due to illness, injury, or medical conditions such as cancer. The app enables users to recreate their unique voice and communicate more naturally through a personalized soundboard.

## Features

Voice Cloning: Orphonos uses advanced AI technology to clone your natural voice from a few sample recordings.

Custom Soundboard: Create a personalized soundboard with frequently used words and phrases, all spoken in your cloned voice.

Quick Communication: Easily access and play your custom phrases for smooth, everyday interactions.

User-Friendly Interface: Intuitive design allows users of all ages to create and manage their soundboard effortlessly.

Privacy First: All voice data is stored securely on your device, ensuring complete privacy.

## How It Works

Record Your Voice: Provide a few sample recordings to help the app understand your unique vocal patterns.

Voice Cloning: The app generates a realistic voice model based on your recordings.

Create Your Soundboard: Add key words, phrases, or sentences you use daily.

Communicate Effortlessly: Tap your soundboard items to play them in your cloned voice whenever you need to speak.

## Why Orphonos?

Losing your voice can be isolating and challenging. Orphonos helps restore a piece of your identity, enabling you to communicate naturally and confidently. Whether for family, friends, or public interactions, your voice matters—and Orphonos helps you keep it.
